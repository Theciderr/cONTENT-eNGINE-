# Research and operator workflow

## Ownership

The assistant owns discovery, research, synthesis, prioritization, drafting, content adaptation, visual preparation, and preparation of the publishing queue. The owner reviews, corrects facts, and approves. Do not require the owner to provide competitor handles, collect research links, design the strategy, write initial drafts, or copy prompts between tools.

The original planning-only scope remains in force. This document specifies the desired operating model; it does not assert that an application, recurring job, or publisher is running.

## Research coverage

Research Instagram and X for:

- Builders showing real software, technical decisions, and practical demos.
- Founders connecting personal work to a recognizable business.
- Agencies explaining websites, automation, lead capture, and follow-up systems.
- Local business owners discussing relevant needs, questions, and objections.

The last group matters: studying only agencies risks optimizing for other sellers rather than buyers. Include smaller relevant operators, not only prominent personalities. Discover accounts autonomously using public search, verified website links, and accessible social material. Never use audience size as proof of client acquisition.

## Research procedure

1. Discover candidate accounts and verify identity through official links where possible.
2. Classify by audience, topic, offer, content format, and relevance to ExpandR's current services.
3. Inspect a bounded sample of accessible recent posts; record the actual sampling window and gaps. A target is up to five posts per account, not a claim that five were accessible.
4. Save post URLs, publication/access dates, exact observations, and available metric timestamps. Distinguish site positioning from post-level findings.
5. Separate repeated patterns from isolated examples. Record counterexamples and avoid inferring which content produced revenue.
6. Recommend original angles connected to the owner's evidence. Mark unverified project claims for targeted confirmation.
7. Deliver a ranked content batch with draft copy, visual concepts, evidence, and approval actions.

If social access fails, use accessible official websites, public indexed excerpts with limited context, and linked public material. Label the gap. Do not pretend website research is a completed Instagram/X feed audit. Request an account connection only when necessary for a concrete operation; do not hand the research assignment back to the owner.

## Review packet

Each batch should contain:

- A short research digest: observed patterns, source links, access gaps, and recommendations.
- Ranked ideas with a target buyer, problem, relevance, and supporting project evidence.
- Complete X drafts and Instagram copy/storyboards or visual previews.
- Only the missing personal facts or disclosure decisions that materially affect a draft.
- Proposed destination and posting time, exact asset versions, and clear approve/revise/reject actions.

The assistant handles routine choices. Ask the owner only for inaccessible personal facts, private-work disclosure, necessary account access, or explicit publishing approval. An earlier answer remains valid within its scope. The owner should approve concrete work, not perform research to make it possible.

## Execution and budget boundary

During active assisted sessions, the assistant performs research and prepares artifacts with available tools. The existing ChatGPT subscription is not assumed to supply a custom application's background API backend. The future application needs a supported execution adapter before it can run unattended; no continuous monitoring is active merely because this plan describes it.

The zero-additional-cost design must not hide an owner-operated copy/paste job inside an “automated” workflow. Prepare and manage research/drafting in the assisted workspace first. If a supported free integration cannot complete a step, show the precise limitation and preserve the ready-to-review work. Do not assume new spending or claim full autonomy across inaccessible services.

Publishing remains approval-bound. Once the exact version, assets, account, and time are approved, a configured supported publisher may execute that action without another routine confirmation. Until such a route is configured and verified, show “approved, awaiting publication” rather than “scheduled” or “published.”
